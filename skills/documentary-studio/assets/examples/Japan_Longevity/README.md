# Example: Why Japanese People Live Longer (Nov 2026)

English-only health-topic film, 4:42 plus a 0:56 Short, warm look, narrated by the producer's own ElevenLabs clone reading English.

- `project.json`: warm look, no wind bed, score with 10 sections, hits and drops, sfx on reveals, Short segments (hook, ikigai, twist).
- `script_master.tsv`: 63 rows, English plus a native Bangla translation (only the English was voiced).
- `shotlist.json`: data graphics with their props: `count` (S02, S57), `bars` (S18), `bowl` (S31), `rank` with per-step `at` times (S49), `card` and `stat`. Real Okinawa and Utsunomiya photos carry `place`.
- `picks.json`: how picks are written ("S29:6" = candidate 6 of shot S29's search, "C5" = Commons candidate 5).
- `thumbnails.json`: three film thumbnails from moments in the film and two vertical Short covers.
- `facts_to_verify.md`: SOURCED, CHECK and CONTESTED claims, math checks, a "not used" list.
- `youtube_publish_kit.md`: scored titles, thumbnail notes, description with sources, hooks, Facebook caption, honesty flags.

Media, renders and keys are not included. Read `../README.md` first: current rules win over this older film.
