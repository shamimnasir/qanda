## Not used (could not be sourced well enough)
- "Okinawan men were number 1 in Japan in 1985": not confirmed.
- "Okinawan women ranked 16th in 2020": not confirmed.
- "82% of Japan's centenarians turned out to be dead": a misreading, so it is left out.
- "Japanese people walk more than other countries": no good comparative source, so the film uses the Utsunomiya tram study.
- Genetics: no claim is made.

## Math and consistency checks
- 107,677 / 153 = 703.8, so "about 700 times". OK.
- 107,677 - 99,763 (2025) = 7,914. Matches the reported rise. OK.
- 87.33 - 87.13 = 0.20 and 81.35 - 81.09 = 0.26 (MHLW rounds unrounded values to +0.25). OK.
- 94,298 + 13,379 = 107,677. OK.
- Kurotani: HR 0.85 = 15% lower. Sone: HR 1.5 = "about one and a half times". Holt-Lunstad: OR 1.50 = "50% higher odds". OK.
- 5th lowest of 47 = 43rd. OK.

## Health-topic honesty flags
- This is not medical advice. The description should say so.
- Most of the evidence is observational (associations, not proof). The script says so once ("A link, not proof") and words the rest as "found", "linked".
- Stock footage of Japanese people and places is illustrative and must not be presented as the specific people in a study.
