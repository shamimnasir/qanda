## 5. Footage honesty flags
- No on-screen illustrative labels (producer decision, 6 Nov 2026). The description now carries the only disclosure that stock is not Picher, because the credits card was also removed. To stay honest without a label, no stock tornado, stained creek or parade appears: the tornado uses real Picher photos, Tar Creek is a graphic, and the parade line uses generic Christmas lights from another city.
- Storm clouds at 0:11 and lightning at about 3:40 are generic stock, not the 2008 storm.
- Children, picnic, sandbox, mine, tunnels, excavators, molten metal, shell casings, lab, signing, moving boxes, cracked ground and digging clips are generic illustration, disclosed in the description and here. None is Picher. Read each clip title in `tracker.csv`.
- FEMA photo at about 3:42: a blown-over tree is from Granby, Missouri, in the same May 2008 storm, and its on-screen credit says so.
- The 1918 photo comes from a National Archives card about explosives found in a building. The film uses the street scene only, and not that claim.
- The cross-section graphics are illustrations, not to scale. The collapse at about 2:52 is not a picture of a real event.
- CC BY-SA photos, used with credit: Kelly K, TheDoctorWho, formulanone, peggydavis66, NevinThompson, Robert Jones. ShareAlike question: **your decision before publishing**. The fully clean swap would be public domain and CC BY images only, which would remove almost all modern Picher street photos.
