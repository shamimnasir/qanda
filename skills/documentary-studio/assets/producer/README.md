# Producer photos for thumbnails

This folder is intentionally empty in the shared version of the skill. Personal photos were removed.

Add your own transparent cutouts here (for example `serious.webp`) and reference them by name in `data/thumbnails.json`: `{"face": "serious", "cx": 960, "top": 90, "height": 1000, "rim": "#ff4f45"}`.

Only use photos of yourself, or people who gave written permission, on your own videos. Made-up or illustrated people related to the film are also fine for thumbnails.
