# Documentary project: instructions for Claude

This folder is one film made with the documentary-studio skill (`__SKILL__`).
Use that skill for any work here; its `references/rules.md` always applies and its `references/playbook.md` is the workflow.
Pipeline: `./studio.sh <step>` (scripts, align, media, download, build, sound, docs, stills, render, qa).

Short version of the rules:
- Never write the long dash character (em or en dash) anywhere. Use commas, periods or hyphens.
- Only commercially licensed assets, each logged in `tracker.csv`. Unclear license: do not use it, flag it. No scraping of Instagram, X, Threads, TikTok or YouTube; other creators' footage only with written permission.
- Never invent facts, sources, numbers or quotes. Never present stock or AI footage as the real place or person.
- Ask before spending money or credits, and state the cost first.
- Always stop at the 3 gates: script, voice, publish.
- Edit: the twist in about 10 s over 3 or 4 shots that follow the voice, no visual over 3 s unless a hold is approved, no "illustrative footage" text, no credits card, credits in the description only.
